The Kitchen

34th Street

Manara Cafe cooks in the Lebanese and wider Mediterranean tradition: grains soaked and cooked from raw, herbs chopped by hand, meat marinated the night before it's grilled. Nothing arrives frozen and pre-portioned.

The dining room is small. The kitchen isn't trying to be everywhere at once, it's trying to get your plate right.
