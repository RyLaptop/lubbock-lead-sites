The Shop

Two chairs. Two barbers. No corporate name on the sign.

Moe and Janet cut hair on 82nd Street, in the same strip they've worked out of for years. There's no receptionist and no upsell script. You sit down, you say what you want, one of them does it.

Fades, tapers, straight-razor edges, kids' cuts. If it's a style a barber can do with clippers and a comb, it's on the list. If you don't know what you want, tell Moe or Janet what you've got going on and they'll tell you what works.
