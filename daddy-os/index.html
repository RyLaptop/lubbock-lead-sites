The Shop

Two days a week

Daddy O's deals in mid-century modern: teak sideboards, walnut credenzas, atomic-starburst clocks, the pieces that made 1958 look like the future. Everything on the floor has already lived one life somewhere else.

The shop keeps a short week on purpose. New inventory lands and gets posted most Wednesdays, then the doors open Friday and Saturday for people who came to actually look at it.
